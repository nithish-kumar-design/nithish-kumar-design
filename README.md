## Design Philosophy

> **Systems over screens.** A well-architected design system multiplies the output of every designer and engineer on a team. I invest in the infrastructure so the product can move fast without breaking consistency.
>
> > **Tokens are the contract.** Design tokens are not a Figma feature — they are the binding agreement between design and engineering. Getting this layer right is the highest-leverage work in any product org.
> >
> > > **Accessibility is not optional.** Building accessible components from the start is always cheaper than retrofitting. I treat WCAG compliance as a baseline engineering constraint, not a feature.
